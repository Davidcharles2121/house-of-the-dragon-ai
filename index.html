How to play

Fly with ↑ / ↓ or W / S, and press SPACE to shoot dragonfire. Every 150 points, choose one of three upgrades. A powerful enemy dragon arrives every 1,000 points with fireball barrages and claw slashes. Collect gold, survive, and evolve your dragon.
